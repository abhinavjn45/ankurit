It’s late. The decision is yours alone.

  • — You can’t tell your co-founder they’re part of the problem.
  • — You can’t tell your investor; it’ll sound like doubt.

So you decide alone.
That is the decision I’m here for.

What a
Thinking Partner is

I am the person you call before you make the call. Sometimes the problem isn’t the decision, It’s trying to think clearly while carrying too much at once. I help you see which ones actually matter.

I don’t tell you what to do. I don’t push opinions or frameworks. I help you slow things down so you can hear what you already know.

In our sessions:

  • — We name the question you’ve been circling but haven't said out loud.
  • — We walk every path, including the one you’ve been avoiding.
  • — We slow down the discussion until the real issue comes into focus.

"You walk in carrying the weight alone. You walk out with a clearer next move."

The Rules of the Room

01. Absolute Confidentiality

No reporting. No leaks. This is a secure space.

02. Total Neutrality

I sit entirely outside your cap table and team dynamics, giving you an unbiased sounding board.

03. Radical Honesty

Drop the founder persona. There is no pressure to perform or have it all figured out.

How it works

01. Rhythm & Pace

Weekly or Biweekly sessions. 60 minutes, over video or in Bangalore. A rhythm that fits the pace of your decisions.

02. 16/6 Access

Same-day response when something breaks, a co-founder blow-up, a term sheet, or a hire falling apart.

03. Compounding Context

We never look at a problem in isolation. I help you recognise your past compromises that are shaping today's reality.

04. Active Pattern Tracking

I help you step back and see your habits, so you catch the blind spots before they happen.
